🌟 What are Positive Yogas?

Yogas are special planetary combinations that produce specific results in life. Positive yogas (also called Shubha Yogas) are auspicious combinations that bring:

  • Raja Yogas - Power, authority, and leadership
  • Dhana Yogas - Wealth and prosperity
  • Pancha Mahapurusha Yogas - Great personality traits
  • Budhi Yogas - Intelligence and wisdom
  • Special Yogas - Various life blessings

These yogas show your natural strengths and the areas where you can excel with proper effort.
