Understanding challenges and their remedies

⚡ What are Doshas & Negative Yogas?

Doshas and negative yogas are challenging planetary combinations that create obstacles or difficulties. However, they are NOT curses or permanent conditions:

  • They indicate karmic lessons to be learned in this lifetime
  • They show areas requiring extra effort and awareness
  • They can be pacified or neutralized through proper remedies
  • Some doshas even become cancelled by other yoga formations

Remember: Awareness is the first step to transformation. Knowing your challenges empowers you to address them proactively.

📿 Remedies & Solutions

Each dosha has specific remedies from classical Vedic texts. Remedies work by:

  • Pacifying malefic influences - Reducing negative effects
  • Karmic balancing - Through charity and service
